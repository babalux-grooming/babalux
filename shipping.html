Customer information

Delivery & Shipping

BABALUX operates in the UK, and products are held in UK stock for delivery to UK customers.

UK mainland delivery rates

Delivery is calculated from the delivery postcode before checkout: £4.99 for the West Midlands region and £6.99 for the rest of mainland England, Scotland and Wales. Enter the postcode for the delivery address in the basket, then use the same postcode at Stripe checkout.

Where orders are dispatched from

Orders will be dispatched from UK stock to eligible UK addresses. Delivery availability and the service offered to a destination will be confirmed before orders open.

Shipping fees and delivery estimates

The delivery charge is £4.99 for postcodes in the West Midlands region and £6.99 for other eligible mainland postcodes in England, Scotland and Wales. The applicable charge is shown in the basket and again at Stripe checkout. Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man and specified offshore island areas are not currently included.

Prices and charges

Product prices are shown in GBP. The applicable delivery charge is added before payment. The checkout page shows the order amount including delivery before the customer confirms payment.

Order updates and tracking

Once the carrier and fulfilment process are confirmed, we will explain whether tracking is provided and how dispatch updates will be sent. These details will be stated before checkout is enabled.

Questions

Please enter the postcode for the intended delivery address to see the correct rate. Customers must use the same postcode for the shipping address on Stripe checkout. Delivery estimates and tracking availability will depend on the carrier and service used.
